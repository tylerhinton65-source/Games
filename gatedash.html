How to play

Your connection is boarding at the far end of the terminal. Get there before the doors close.

  • SWIPE ◀ ▶Change lanes around walkers, carts and crowds
  • TAP / ▲Hop over rolling bags and wet-floor signs
  • SNACKSPoints. Grab them in a row to build a multiplier
  • COFFEE+2 seconds and a sprint, but it fills your bladder
  • WATER+1 second. Also fills your bladder, a little
  • RESTROOMBladder full? You run at half speed. Run onto the blue spot by a restroom door to duck in. The clock keeps running while you go
  • WALKERSSome travelers walk your way. You catch up to them slower than everything else
  • CREWPilots and flight attendants march in single file. Pass a whole crew cleanly for bonus points
  • GATE CHGYour gate can move mid-run. You get a little extra time, but not much
  • WALKWAYMoving walkways carry you faster
  • SODA+1 second and a quick sugar rush, but it fills your bladder fast
  • CARTSCourtesy carts beep and swerve into the next lane. Watch where they settle
  • BENJIBring him for +4 s every gate. When his meter fills, find a pet relief area
  • BINBetween airports, drag all 4 bags into the overhead bin before it closes
  • PAUSETap the pause button in the corner, or press P or Esc
  • STROLLERPush Caleb in his stroller. No hopping and slower turns
  • LAURENThe final challenge: a bladder half the size, no hopping, and surprise diaper changes. Reach a restroom before the countdown ends, or change him on the spot
