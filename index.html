    Lauren's Cabin

    Lauren just got the keys to an old cabin up in the mountains! It's packed with dusty old stuff, and Benji's here to help.

    Drag two matching things together to merge them. Drop a match onto a cobwebbed thing to free it, and merge next to a box to open it. Use what you make to turn it into your dream cabin.
    Lauren'sCabin

    How to play

    ✨ Tap the glowing things (the toolbox, cleaning cart and more) to make new stuff.

    🤝 Drag two matching things together to merge them into something better.

    🕸️ Cobwebbed things are stuck. Drop a matching item on one to free it.

    📦 Boxes open when you merge something right next to them.

    🏡 Each job needs certain items. When you have them, tap Fix it! and watch Lauren fix up the cabin.

    🐾 Finish a whole area to unlock the next one, and a new pup moves in.

    🗑️ Board too full? Merge things, or tap something you don't need and toss it. Everything saves on its own.

    Start over?

    This clears the board and all the jobs so you can fix up the cabin again from the very beginning. It can't be undone.
