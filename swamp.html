How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to aim. The gator fires while your finger is down. Your finger doesn't need to be on the zombies.
  • Or tap 🎯 AUTO and he'll aim himself.
  • Tap a gun at the top to switch. Tap the gun you're holding to reload early.
  • Zombies drop coins. Between waves, spend them in the 🛒 Shop, or tap a glowing spot to build a turret.
  • The fence holds zombies back. ✈️ Airstrike recharges during waves. Every 5th wave brings a boss.
